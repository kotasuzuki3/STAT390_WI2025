I worked on matching the extracted tissue slices from Case 25-30. 

In Case 25, there were tissue slices that were very closely laid out back-to-back. This made Cara's automated code extract two separate slices as one image. To fix this, I changed this part of Cara's code: 

double distanceThreshold = 5000.0

By decreasing this threshold value, I made sure that each tissue slice was extracted invidually. Other than this, I had no other issue matching the three stain types and uploading them onto OneDrive.
